STYLE • 5 MIN READ

10 modern living room ideas for a calm home

A balanced living room starts with a clear purpose. Use a simple colour palette, comfortable furniture and one or two strong focal points.

Read article

Start with the largest pieces first. Keep walking areas open, then add lighting, rugs, artwork and greenery. Natural textures such as wood and linen can make a modern room feel warmer without making it crowded.

Most importantly, choose pieces that work for the people who use the room every day.

MATERIALS • 4 MIN READ

How to choose the right flooring

Think about durability, maintenance, room use and the visual mood you want before choosing a floor.

Read article

Wood gives warmth, stone can feel premium, and tiles are practical in areas where moisture or easy cleaning is important. Always consider how the flooring connects visually with nearby rooms.

BEDROOM • 4 MIN READ

Make a small bedroom feel bigger

Good layout and visual simplicity can make a compact bedroom feel comfortable and organised.

Read article

Use furniture that fits the room, keep storage organised and let natural light reach as much of the room as possible. Mirrors can also help reflect light and create a sense of openness.

KITCHEN • 5 MIN READ

Build a kitchen material palette

Choose cabinets, counters, backsplash and hardware as a connected visual system.

Read article

Pick one main material, one supporting texture and a small number of accent details. This keeps the kitchen cohesive and makes future changes easier.
